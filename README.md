· **Niche:** Local towing, Flatbed & Roadside assistance (Chicago, IL, USA)
· **Period analysed:** 3 September - 13 November 2026 (~10 weeks)
· **Data source:** Google Search Console (Web search)

## 1. Visibility step-change from 10 October

Impressions averaged about 17/day before 10 Oct and about 261/day after it. They have held between roughly 150 and 430 per day since.
- This is because of optimized  service pages, location pages and blog posts architected and deployed inside GoHighLevel (GHL) targeting Chicago neighborhoods and suburbs for emergency towing and roadside assistance.
- Executed advanced On-Page SEO, Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) strategies, integrating semantic heading hierarchies, local keyword clusters and structured data schema.
-  **Results:** Driven by comprehensive page roll-out and topical content expansion, the website achieved 9,761 total organic search impressions and 108 clicks during 2 months, successfully capturing high-intent local search traffic across Chicago.

## 2. CTR and position: an honest read

Visibility grew mostly through informational, long-tail queries such as "find my towed car" and "which company towed my car Chicago". Average position therefore widened from about 7.8 to about 14, and CTR normalized from the very small early-September sample. Clicks per day held steady and then rose in November (2.23/day, up from about 1.3/day).

## 3. Where the visibility comes from

Blog content earns most impressions (7,242 across 93 URLs). The homepage earns the most clicks (68). Service and service-area pages were published in mid-October and are still building traffic.

## Next steps

1. Lift service and location pages out of positions 15-50 for commercial terms such as "emergency towing", "roadside assistance Chicago" and "24 hour towing Chicago" (currently 0 clicks).
2. Add internal links and clear calls to action from high-impression blog posts to the service pages.
3. Re-write titles and meta descriptions on the posts with high impressions and low CTR.
4. Set up GA4 call and form conversion tracking so clicks can be tied to leads.
